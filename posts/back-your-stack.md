BackYourStack analyzes public repositories or uploaded dependency files to identify which open source dependencies in a stack are looking for funding. It started as an Open Collective project and now points users toward the newer Ecosystem Funds model, but it still demonstrates a practical workflow for funding the dependencies a company relies on.<!--more-->
