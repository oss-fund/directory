Bepro lets organizations create task-based marketplaces where contributors can work on development, marketing, writing, or other scoped tasks and get paid in tokens or stablecoins. It is relevant to OSS.Fund as a live blockchain-native bounty system, although the current marketplace is broader than open source alone.<!--more-->
