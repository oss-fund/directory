Bounties Network combines a task marketplace with Ethereum-based staking and arbitration, aiming to let people fund work without relying on a centralized platform operator. It remains one of the clearer examples of a decentralized bounty protocol, even though the public site now emphasizes protocol components and explorer flows more than transparent marketplace scale.<!--more-->
