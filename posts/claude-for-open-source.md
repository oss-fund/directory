Claude for Open Source provides approved open-source maintainers and contributors with six months of Claude Max 20x as an individual subscription. It is software access, not cash funding or a team license.<!--more-->

**Who can apply.** Applicants must meet at least one program track: maintain packages with 500 or more dependent repositories, 100 or more dependent packages, or 200,000 or more combined monthly downloads; be a listed committer or maintainer of a recognized foundation or language project; have 100 or more pull requests merged into public repositories they do not own in the prior 12 months; maintain a repository with 20 or more unique external contributors whose pull requests were merged in that period; or maintain a repository with an OpenSSF criticality score of at least 0.4. Applicants who miss those thresholds may apply under the discretionary Ecosystem Impact track if they maintain or significantly contribute to a project that the open-source ecosystem meaningfully depends on. All applicants must be individuals at least the greater of 18 or the age of majority in their jurisdiction, legally resident where Claude.ai is offered and not subject to U.S. export or sanctions restrictions, with a GitHub account in good standing that is at least two years old, public OSS activity in the prior 90 days, and work on at least one OSI-licensed project. Anthropic employees, contractors, agents, and their immediate or household family members are excluded. Meeting a threshold does not guarantee approval.

**Application and availability.** The program page currently invites applications. Apply through the [official Claude for Open Source page](https://claude.com/contact-sales/claude-for-oss), sign in with GitHub, provide an email and a short intended-use description, and explain in up to 500 words why you qualify. Anthropic reviews applications on a rolling basis, gives no response timeline, and says it contacts approved applicants only. The terms set a cap of 10,000 approved recipients unless Anthropic increases it; the application period has no stated closing date but may be ended at Anthropic's discretion. Approved applicants must activate within 90 days after receiving the gift code.

**Terms to check.** The benefit is personal, non-transferable, and has no cash value. The base subscription is free during the six-month period, but the terms allow charges for overages. It does not renew automatically. Free accounts return to the Free plan; an existing paid subscription resumes afterward unless canceled. The program page describes the prior plan and rate resuming, while the terms say the then-current plan and rate; confirm the billing details before applying. Recipients are responsible for any tax obligations.

See the [official program page](https://claude.com/contact-sales/claude-for-oss) and [terms and conditions](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-for-oss-terms).
