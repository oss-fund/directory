CodeRabbit provides free code review and related tools for public repositories, helping maintainers assess proposed changes. It separately sponsors selected maintainers with cash.<!--more-->

**Eligibility and access.** CodeRabbit's current open-source page offers free OSS access for public repositories, with no application or approval queue. Maintainers sign up with GitHub or GitLab, install CodeRabbit, and enable it for the repository. Repositories with fewer than 10 stars need a manual review trigger; the published OSS PR review allowance is 1–10 per developer per hour, scoped per repository and adjusted by project community and popularity. The page lists AI reviews, slop detection, triage, and Change Stack; Discord support is included for large open-source projects.

**Security features.** CodeRabbit's plans page reserves continuous pull-request security review for Advanced. Deep Scan is usage-priced separately, so the OSS offer should not be read as including continuous security reviews or unlimited scans.

**Cash sponsorships.** These are separate from the free product offer. CodeRabbit says it selects and funds maintainers proactively through GitHub Sponsors, direct invoices, and Open Collective. Its current page does not publish cash-sponsorship eligibility criteria or an application route. A 2025 announcement links to a sponsorship form, but that form could not be inspected, so its current availability and requirements are unconfirmed. Do not treat product access as a cash award.

**Terms and limits.** CodeRabbit's terms say AI outputs may be incomplete or inaccurate and the service is provided as available; the terms also reserve the ability to modify or terminate free features. Maintainers should review automated findings themselves. CodeRabbit does not promise a cash award or a response timeline for sponsorships.

See the [current OSS program and tracker](https://www.coderabbit.ai/oss), [plans and pricing](https://docs.coderabbit.ai/management/plans), [FAQ](https://www.coderabbit.ai/faq), [terms](https://www.coderabbit.ai/legal/terms-of-service), and the [2025 sponsorship announcement and linked form](https://www.coderabbit.ai/blog/coderabbit-commits-1-million-to-open-source).
