CommitChange packages donation processing, campaign pages, donor records, events, and fundraising support into one hosted system for nonprofits and social-impact organizations. Its positioning is closer to an all-in-one fundraising stack than to a simple crowdfunding tool, so the current value is best understood as donation infrastructure plus campaign operations.<!--more-->
