Ellcrys proposed a blockchain-based alternative to centralized code hosting, where contributors could co-own software, participate in governance, and share revenue from the products they helped build. The project now appears dormant, but it remains a good historical example of attempts to merge decentralized infrastructure with software monetization.<!--more-->
