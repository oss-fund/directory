FOSS Pay was a self-hosted donation collection platform for maintainers and groups that wanted to accept contributions on their own infrastructure. It focused on a minimal open source stack around Python, PostgreSQL, and Stripe instead of a multi-tenant hosted marketplace.<!--more-->
