FOSSA, the Free and Open Source Software Audit project, was created by the European Union to improve the security of widely used open source infrastructure through audits, hackathons, and bug-bounty programmes. The programme has ended, but it remains an important historical example of government-backed grant and bounty funding for open source security.<!--more-->
