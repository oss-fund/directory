Gitcoin has moved away from being primarily known for bounties and is now best understood as a web3 public-goods funding network centered on grants rounds and other plural funding mechanisms. It remains highly relevant to open source and developer infrastructure funding, but the current product is much more about grants coordination than about individual issue rewards.<!--more-->
