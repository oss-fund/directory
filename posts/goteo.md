Goteo focuses on crowdfunding for projects that claim a public, commons, or social-return dimension rather than purely private upside. It also supports matchfunding and open-data features, which makes it one of the clearer examples of a mission-driven crowdfunding platform that overlaps with open knowledge and open source values.<!--more-->
