Gumroad gives creators a lightweight storefront for selling ebooks, courses, templates, memberships, and other digital goods without building their own checkout stack. It is a mainstream creator platform rather than an OSS-specific service, but maintainers can use it to monetize educational content, guides, and other project-adjacent products.<!--more-->
