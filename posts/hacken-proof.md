HackenProof is focused on security work rather than general OSS contribution funding: companies launch bug bounty programmes, researchers submit findings, and rewards are paid out based on validated vulnerabilities. It is still worth listing because security bounties are a real funding path for some open source-adjacent infrastructure and blockchain projects, but it is not an OSS-specific platform.<!--more-->
