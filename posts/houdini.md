Houdini is not a donation marketplace; it is infrastructure that nonprofits can deploy themselves to run campaigns, recurring donations, events, CRM-style donor management, and payouts. That makes it a stronger fit under operational support than under direct funding, because its primary value is reducing the cost of building and operating fundraising systems.<!--more-->
