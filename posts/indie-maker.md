Indiemaker is not aimed specifically at open source, but it is relevant as a marketplace for buying and selling internet businesses and side projects that may themselves include developer tools, SaaS products, or communities. For maintainers, the key analogy is not donations or bounties but the idea of selling or transferring ownership of a software asset outright.<!--more-->
