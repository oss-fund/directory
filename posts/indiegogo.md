Indiegogo lets creators raise money before shipping a product and then continue selling through its InDemand program after the main campaign ends. It is not an OSS-specific platform, but it remains one of the largest general-purpose crowdfunding examples in the directory and a useful benchmark for campaign-based funding models.<!--more-->
