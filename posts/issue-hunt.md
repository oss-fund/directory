IssueHunt is built around GitHub issue workflows: maintainers register repositories, supporters add money to specific issues, and contributors claim rewards after their pull requests are accepted. It remains one of the clearest examples of a purpose-built OSS bounty platform, even if much of the recent activity visible on individual issue pages is relatively sparse.<!--more-->
