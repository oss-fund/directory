IT Sphere described itself as a blockchain-based platform for connecting product developers, outsourcing teams, investors, and other technical participants. The service is discontinued, and the surviving evidence is thin, but it appears to have been closer to a crypto fundraising ecosystem than to a conventional issue-bounty platform.<!--more-->
