Katalyse, formerly FundYourselfNow, repositioned itself as a launch and listing platform for ICOs and other tokenized fundraising campaigns. The service is discontinued, but it remains relevant as a historical example of blockchain-native crowdfunding infrastructure rather than a conventional OSS bounty marketplace.<!--more-->
