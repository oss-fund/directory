Kauri tried to build a crypto-native documentation network where Ethereum educators and technical writers could earn rewards for tutorials, guides, and curated knowledge. The live product has been shut down and turned into an archive, but it remains a useful example of content monetization built around developer education rather than around code sponsorship directly.<!--more-->
