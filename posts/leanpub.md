Leanpub is aimed at technical authors who want to publish iteratively, take reader feedback early, and sell books or courses without managing their own storefront. It is a mainstream creator platform rather than an OSS-specific tool, but maintainers can use it to monetize books, manuals, and educational material around their projects.<!--more-->
