License Zero automated part of the dual-licensing workflow for developers who wanted to publish code under an open source license while selling separate commercial-use permissions for closed deployments. The service now appears inactive, but it remains a notable example of legal and payment tooling aimed specifically at open source licensing-based revenue.<!--more-->
