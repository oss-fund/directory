NLnet is a Dutch non-profit foundation funding open internet technologies and digital commons. Its grants provide cost recovery rather than commercial rates. Depending on the fund, grantees can also request support such as security and accessibility audits, testing, licensing advice, and mentoring.<!--more-->

As checked on 29 October 2026, [Restack](https://nlnet.nl/restack/) and [CodeSupply](https://nlnet.nl/codesupply/) are accepting proposals, with a deadline of 3 November 2026 at 12:00 CET. Check the [funding page](https://nlnet.nl/funding.html) for subsequent calls and each fund's current conditions.

Individuals and organizations can apply, subject to fund-specific eligibility. Restack and CodeSupply require a strong European dimension, normally demonstrated by allocating at least 60% of funds to team members from Horizon Europe-eligible countries. Exceptional-quality proposals from elsewhere may be considered where the proposer has unique technical expertise. Funded software and hardware must be published in full under recognized free and open source licenses; scientific results must be open access.

Read the [current GenAI policy](https://nlnet.nl/foundation/policies/generativeAI/2.0/) and its [FAQ](https://nlnet.nl/foundation/policies/generativeAI/faq/) before applying. Applicants must write their own proposals, with disclosed accessibility exceptions during the application process. GenAI must not produce funded code, designs, or documentation; read-only review is allowed. Restack also excludes AI-related projects unless they already have more than one million active human users and are directly relevant to the stack.
