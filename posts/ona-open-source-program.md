Ona for Open Source offers selected maintainers and core contributors monthly credits for using Ona's AI development platform. The official page advertises up to $200 per month, with the amount tied to the degree of open-source contribution. This reduces tooling costs rather than providing cash funding.<!--more-->

**Eligibility and application.** Applicants must maintain or be a core contributor to at least one active, well-established open-source project. Apply through the linked form with a GitHub profile and the repositories maintained or contributed to. Ona says it reviews applications and verifies eligibility, with most processed within a few days. Approval is not guaranteed.

**Availability and terms.** The program page currently invites applications. It does not specify an application deadline, credit duration, expiration, or detailed usage limits. The linked application form could not be inspected in this review, so additional requirements may apply. The general [Terms of Service](https://ona.com/legal/terms-of-service) govern service use; check the program page and terms before relying on credits.
