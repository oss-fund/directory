The Open Source Endowment (OSE) is a US 501(c)(3) community endowment that invests donations and spends only the investment returns on microgrants of up to $5,000 to critical, independent open source projects. Grantees are identified by an open, data-driven [selection model](https://github.com/osendowment/model) and approved by the board; there is no application form, but anyone can [nominate a project](https://endowment.dev/funding/), including their own.<!--more-->
