The Open Source Endowment (OSE) is a US 501(c)(3) community endowment that invests donations and spends only the investment returns on microgrants of up to $5,000 to critical, independent open source projects. OSE uses an open [selection model](https://github.com/osendowment/model) to identify critical projects and contacts top-scoring projects to check eligibility and interest. The board approves the final grants. There is no application form. [Community nominations](https://endowment.dev/grants/) help OSE check which projects its model covers and do not affect grant rankings.<!--more-->
