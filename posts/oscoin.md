Oscoin aimed to rethink how open source collaboration, reputation, and value sharing could work if the underlying coordination layer were decentralized from the start. The project now appears dormant, but it remains relevant as a historical example of attempts to treat OSS sustainability as an infrastructure and protocol problem rather than only as a donation problem.<!--more-->
