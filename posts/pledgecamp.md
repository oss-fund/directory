Pledgecamp's pitch is that crowdfunding should be more transparent and safer for backers by combining escrow, milestone-based releases, and blockchain-backed insurance mechanics. The technical documentation is still present and active, but the product today reads more like an ongoing protocol and platform build than like a high-volume mainstream crowdfunding marketplace.<!--more-->
