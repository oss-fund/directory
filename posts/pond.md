Pond is a startup-launch platform aimed at AI and Web3 teams that want distribution, hackathon participation, and bounty-style contributions from external builders. It is not specifically an open source platform, but its bounty mechanics and developer-facing programs overlap with the kinds of contribution incentives some OSS projects may care about.<!--more-->
