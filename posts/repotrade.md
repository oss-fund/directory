Repo.trade is building a tokenized funding model for open source repositories: supporters send USDC into an auction, receive repository tokens, and then trade those tokens on a Solana-based market. The pitch is that repository owners get immediate funding plus an ongoing share of trading fees if their token sees continued activity.<!--more-->
