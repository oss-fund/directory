Saasify bundled common SaaS boilerplate such as auth, billing, docs, and marketing pages so developers could commercialize APIs and serverless functions more quickly. The hosted platform is discontinued, but it remains a useful historical example of tooling built specifically around turning developer projects into paid services.<!--more-->
