Software Freedom Conservancy acts as a nonprofit home for free and open source projects that want fiscal sponsorship, legal support, bookkeeping, fundraising infrastructure, and administrative help without creating their own standalone organization. For many established OSS projects, its value is less about a single donation button and more about long-term operational support around money, compliance, contracts, and project governance.<!--more-->
