SourceCred analyzes activity across platforms such as GitHub, Discord, and Discourse to assign transparent contribution scores called Cred. Communities can then use those scores to distribute a project-specific reward currency called Grain, which makes SourceCred more like contribution-accounting infrastructure than a simple donations platform.<!--more-->
