Upwork is a broad marketplace for contract work, consulting, and project-based hiring across software, design, writing, support, and many other functions. It is not OSS-specific, but maintainers and open source teams can use it to sell implementation, maintenance, documentation, or advisory services around their projects.<!--more-->
